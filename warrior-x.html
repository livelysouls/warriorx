Who We Are

Discipline.
Power.
Excellence.

Established in May 2022, The Warrior X is part of the Warriors Martial Arts Karate Academy — one of Lucknow's most respected training institutions. We are recognised by the Karate Association of U.P. and affiliated with the Karate India Organisation.

Our training is rooted in the Shotokan style — a traditional discipline focused on self-defence and personal transformation. From your first white belt to black belt mastery, every step is guided by our world-class coaches.

Affiliated with the United Shotokan Association, KAUP, and LDKA — we bring national-level expertise to your neighbourhood.
